THE MAGIC NEVER ENDS.

THE MAN BEHIND THE MAGIC

Small in stature.
Giant in history.

Born in Rosario, Argentina, Lionel Andrés Messi grew into one of the most celebrated footballers the world has ever seen. His balance, vision, close control and remarkable consistency made him a defining player of his generation.

From his breakthrough at Barcelona to international glory with Argentina, Messi's journey has been filled with extraordinary goals, difficult setbacks, unforgettable comebacks and historic achievements.

His story is not only about winning. It is about persistence, teamwork, creativity and the joy of playing football.

MILESTONES THAT MADE HISTORY

A timeline of greatness.

From a young dreamer in Rosario to a world champion.

1987

The beginning

Lionel Messi is born on 24 June in Rosario, Argentina.

2004

Barcelona debut

Messi makes his official first-team debut for FC Barcelona as a teenager.

2009

A historic season

Barcelona wins the Champions League as part of a remarkable six-trophy calendar year.

2012

Record-breaking goals

Messi scores 91 goals in official senior matches for club and country during the calendar year, according to widely cited records.

2015

Another European crown

Barcelona wins a second treble in Messi's career, featuring the league, cup and Champions League.

2021

International breakthrough

Messi captains Argentina to victory in the Copa América, his first major senior international title with the national team.

2022

The dream comes true

Messi leads Argentina to the FIFA World Cup title in Qatar and wins the tournament's Golden Ball.

2023

A new chapter in Miami

Messi joins Inter Miami and helps the club win the Leagues Cup in his first season.

2024

Back-to-back continental glory

Argentina wins the Copa América again, extending Messi's international legacy.

The art of Messi.

His game is a blend of intelligence, technique and creativity.

Close dribbling

Passing magic

03 / FINISHING

Clinical left foot

04 / INTELLIGENCE

Football IQ
