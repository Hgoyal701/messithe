2021

Copa América glory

2021

2022

Argentina defeats France on penalties after a 3–3 final to become world champions.

Messi joins Inter Miami and wins the Leagues Cup in his first season.

QATAR · 18 DECEMBER 2022

THE NIGHT THE DREAM CAME TRUE.

Argentina and France produced one of the most memorable finals in World Cup history. After extra time, the score was 3–3. Argentina won the penalty shootout 4–2.

Messi scored twice in the final and was named the tournament's best player. Argentina claimed its third men's World Cup title.
