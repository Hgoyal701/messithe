THE CAREER ARCHIVE

UNLOCK THE
WHOLE JOURNEY.

Open the archive and travel through the defining chapters of Messi's career.

1987

Rosario, Argentina

Lionel Andrés Messi is born on 24 June.

2000

A move to Barcelona

Moves to Spain as a teenager and joins Barcelona's youth setup.

2004

First-team debut

Makes his official Barcelona first-team debut in October.

2005

Youth world champion

Wins the FIFA World Youth Championship with Argentina.

2009

European glory

Wins the Champions League and his first Ballon d'Or.

2011

Another European crown

Barcelona defeats Manchester United in the Champions League final.

2012

A historic year

Scores 91 goals across official matches in the calendar year, according to the widely recognised record.

2015

The treble again

Barcelona wins La Liga, the Copa del Rey and the Champions League.

2019

Another Ballon d'Or

Wins his sixth Ballon d'Or after another exceptional season.

2021

Copa América champion

Argentina wins the Copa América, Messi's first senior major international trophy.

2021

A Paris chapter

Joins Paris Saint-Germain after leaving Barcelona.

2022

World champion

Argentina defeats France on penalties in a memorable World Cup final.

2023

Miami begins

Joins Inter Miami and begins a new chapter in Major League Soccer.

2024

Continental champions again

Argentina wins a second consecutive Copa América title.

NOW

The story continues

Follow official club and national-team sources for the latest updates.
