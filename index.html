THE FULL STORY

EVERY ERA. EVERY CHAPTER.

Unlock the timeline from the early days in Rosario to the world champion era.

1987

The beginning

Born on 24 June in Rosario, Argentina.

2000

A new home

Moves to Barcelona as a teenager and joins the club's youth system.

2004

First-team debut

Makes his official first-team debut for Barcelona in October 2004.

2005

Argentina breakthrough

Wins the FIFA World Youth Championship with Argentina's under-20 side.

2009

European champion

Wins the Champions League with Barcelona and his first Ballon d'Or.

2012

A historic scoring year

Sets the celebrated calendar-year scoring record with 91 goals.

2015

Another treble

Barcelona wins the treble of La Liga, Copa del Rey and Champions League.

2021

Copa América glory

Wins his first major senior international trophy with Argentina.

2022

World champion

Argentina beats France on penalties after a 3–3 World Cup final.

2023

A new adventure

Begins his Inter Miami chapter in Major League Soccer.

2024

Continental champions

Argentina wins the Copa América, defending its title.
