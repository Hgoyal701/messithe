01 / Beyond football
10
A LEGACY IN BLUE & WHITE

MORE THAN
A PLAYER.

There are footballers who entertain, footballers who win, and footballers who change the way the game is understood. Lionel Messi belongs in that rare conversation.

His game is built on balance, close control, vision and an extraordinary ability to find space where none appears to exist. He can glide past defenders, thread a pass through a crowded defence, or change a match with a single touch.

His journey was not a straight line. It included early challenges, painful international defeats, difficult decisions and years of expectation. The defining feature of his story is not that he avoided setbacks, but that he kept returning to the pitch.

05 / The ultimate prize

THE NIGHT
THAT BECAME HISTORY.

For years, the World Cup represented the great unfinished chapter in Messi's international story. Argentina's triumph in Qatar brought that journey to a remarkable conclusion.

The final against France finished 3–3 after extra time. Argentina won the penalty shootout 4–2, securing their third men's World Cup title. Messi scored twice in the final and received the Golden Ball as the tournament's best player.

Beyond the result, the victory was the culmination of a generation's effort, a captain's persistence and a nation's long-held dream.

07 / The football

WHAT MAKES
HIM SPECIAL?

His game is not built on one skill. It is the combination of several qualities, working together at extraordinary speed.

01

CLOSE CONTROL

Messi keeps the ball close to his feet, allowing quick changes of direction and tight turns even when defenders close him down.

02

VISION

He spots passing lanes early and can deliver a decisive pass before the defence realises the opportunity is there.

03

FINISHING

From curled shots to delicate chips, he has developed a broad range of ways to turn chances into goals.

04

DRIBBLING

Low centre of gravity, balance and timing help him move past opponents without relying on elaborate tricks.

05

PLAYMAKING

He often drops deeper to help build attacks, draw defenders out of position and create openings for teammates.

06

COMPOSURE

His calm decision-making in crowded spaces gives him the ability to make difficult football actions look simple.
